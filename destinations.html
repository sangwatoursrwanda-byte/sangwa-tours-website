Places to discover

A few of the many places that make Rwanda special.

Volcanoes National Park

Known for mountain gorillas, volcanic peaks and beautiful high-altitude scenery in northwestern Rwanda.

Nyungwe National Park

A large montane rainforest known for biodiversity, primates, birds and the canopy walkway.

Akagera National Park

Rwanda's savannah park, with lakes, wetlands and opportunities for wildlife viewing.

Lake Kivu

A scenic lake in western Rwanda, ideal for relaxing, lakeside views and selected water activities.
